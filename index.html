Shared product terminology

Glossary

Business and engineering definitions
Use these definitions as the shared vocabulary across Product, Engineering, Operations, and Billing teams.
TermBusiness definitionEngineering meaning
Time to Policy ReviewHow quickly an identified policy is reviewed.Elapsed time from policy identification to completion of policy review.
Time to Billing UpdateHow quickly a validated policy change reaches the billing update process.Elapsed time from completed policy review/validation to billing update handoff.
End-to-End TimeTotal time from policy identification to billing update.Elapsed time from the initial identified event through billing update handoff.
On-Time Billing UpdateBilling update completed before the policy becomes effective.Billing update timestamp is earlier than the policy effective date.
Late Billing UpdateBilling update completed after the policy becomes effective.Billing update timestamp is later than the policy effective date.
Practice-Reported RuleA billing rule or issue reported by the practice.Imported business-reported record, optionally linked to a Jira reference.
Tool-Detected RuleA rule identified proactively by Payer Surveillance.Policy-derived rule that meets the product's defined billing/actionability criteria.
Potentially Missed RuleA practice-reported rule not identified by the tool.A reported rule with no matching tool-detected rule in the comparison dataset.
Actionable PolicyA policy change that requires a billing or workflow action.Policy classified as actionable according to the standardized taxonomy and validation outcome.
Policy Effective DateDate from which the payer policy change applies.Reference date used for priority and on-time billing calculations.
ClassificationBusiness category such as Billing, Authorization, or Documentation.Standardized policy taxonomy category assigned by the surveillance workflow.
